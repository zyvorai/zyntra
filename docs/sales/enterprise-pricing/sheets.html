Explainable decisions. Accountable execution.
Community

Free for evaluation and non-production use under the Zyvor Production License v1.0: the decision engine (gaps, explainable what-if with ranges, ranked plan, constraints), industry packs with file, HTTP and webhook sources, the console, quorum approvals with OIDC and change windows, signed decision records with outcome checks, and Gravia, webhook and file executors.

Enterprise

Production rights plus multi-cluster decision control, SAML and SCIM via Haven, auto-approve policy, durable decision history and scorecards, learned edge weights, ITSM and paging connectors, certified packs and releases, and accountable support.

Commercial recommendation

  • Sell Zyntra Enterprise as an annual subscription priced by managed clusters and KPI graphs. A shop or a plant line on a pack is one KPI graph.
  • Do not charge by user, KPI sample, API call, action executed, or dashboard seat.
  • Keep the existing capabilities in Community. Do not restrict the current repository.
  • Lead with a paid production pilot on one cluster and credit it against year one on conversion.
  • Bundle with Netra, Gravia, and Fabric Keep as the Zyvor decision loop; sell packs on their own where there is no Zyvor estate.

Ship plan and product boundary

Ship what you can support

The commercial proposal should reflect what is actually ready to operate and support.

Now: Community

Decision engine with ranges, pairs and constraints, the shop pack and generic sources, OIDC and quorum approvals, signed decision records, Gravia, webhook and file executors, Keep execution.

Enterprise v1

Multi-cluster plan, SAML/SCIM via Haven, auto-approve policy, durable decision history and scorecards, ITSM connectors, certified packs (shop, plant, GPU), certified build pipeline, and support operations.

Post-GA

Learned edge weights, second-wave packs, MSP multi-tenancy, and white-label after technical and commercial demand is proven.

Release gates before the first Enterprise contract

  • Define Community and Enterprise build provenance and commercial licence enforcement under the Zyvor Production License.
  • Publish a support matrix for Kubernetes versions, Netra, Gravia, Fabric and Keep releases, model gateways, and supported packs.
  • Create signed release artifacts, SBOMs, a CVE response policy, upgrade/rollback procedures, and an enterprise release channel.
  • Verify Keep brokered execution end to end on the reference lab, including sandbox egress to the broker.
  • Establish support intake, severity definitions, on-call ownership, escalation paths, and customer communication templates.
  • Attach a module availability schedule to every proposal. Do not contract learned weights or MSP tenancy before they are deliverable.
